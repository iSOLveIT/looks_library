New to color grading? Read this first
Node
One step of correction in Resolve. Serial nodes are applied one after another. Parallel nodes are applied side by side and blended together.
Lift, Gamma, Gain
Three wheels that control the shadows (Lift), midtones (Gamma) and highlights (Gain).
Contrast
The difference between the darkest and brightest parts. More contrast looks punchier; less looks softer.
Saturation
How strong colors are. In Resolve the slider starts at 50, which means no change. 0 is black and white and 100 is double.
Hue
The color itself (red, orange, teal, and so on), separate from how bright or strong it is.
Color cast / tint
A subtle wash of one color over the image, such as a warm amber glow or a cool blue chill.
Waveform
A scope showing brightness. The bottom is black and the top is white.
Vectorscope
A scope showing color. Healthy skin lands near the diagonal skin-tone line, whatever the person’s skin color.
Qualifier / Power Window
Tools that select part of the image, either by color or brightness (Qualifier) or by a drawn shape (Power Window), so you can change only that part.
LUT
A saved color preset you can apply in one click.
